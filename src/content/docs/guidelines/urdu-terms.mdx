Urdu translations for common terms used in interfaces and content of digital products. 

These translations are opinionated and favors a simpler direct translation, instead of adding conjugate verbs like ‘ہے’ and ‘کریں’. 

Sometime the Urdu transliteration works better than the translation of the term.
