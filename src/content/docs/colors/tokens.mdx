Digital Design Nizam uses stock Tailwind color as well as a custom color set called 'Nizam Green'. 

These color are applied semantically under the `Theme` tokens ccollection. It is recommended that you use these tokens to get started. This will also make sure that your designs support easy dark mode swithcing and complies with WCAG 2.1 AA contrast standards.

- `Foreground` for text and icon
- `Background` for pages and screens
- `Surface` for highlights and cards
- `Muted` for supportive text or disabled
- `Primary` and Secondary to represent the main actions
- `Success`, `Danger` and `Warning` for alerts and notifications
- `Primary Accent` and `Secondary Accent` for highlights and accents
